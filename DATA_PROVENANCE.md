# Data provenance and publication safety

This repository uses only deterministic synthetic data generated by `src/delivery_risk/synthetic.py`.

No employer data, customer records, internal queries, table names, schemas, server paths, credentials, operational thresholds, colleague names, or non-public results were used. Warehouse, carrier, order, and customer attributes are fictional. The generator was designed independently to exercise time-based prediction, calibration, and capacity-constrained ranking.

Synthetic evaluation results demonstrate that the implementation works on its own controlled fixture. They do not estimate production accuracy, preventable delays, service impact, or financial value.
